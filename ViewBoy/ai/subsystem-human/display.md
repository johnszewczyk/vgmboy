# Display

ViewBoy uses a locally bundled Doto display face and a blue phosphor palette.
The interface remains in WebKit and CSS; a transparent, click-through Metal
overlay adds scanline attenuation without changing layout or controls.

- `Sources/ViewBoy/Resources/styles.css`
